Lede
The story opens with an anecdotal lede of a man who, while driving in a remote region of Colorado, veered off a cliff, landing in a ravine. The lede continues by explaining the tumultuous and variable-filled endeavor to both rescue the man, provide a blood transfusion at the site of rescue, and transport him to the nearest trauma center. While this analysis will go on to reveal my criticism of what—and who—this article leaves out, regarding where it succeeds, the opening is captivating. Furthermore, the photographs embedded into this story provide visualization for the scope of remoteness the article will go onto discuss. For example, the image of St. Mary’s Medical Center by Smiley N. Pool: The Rocky Mountains dominate the landscape, crushing the hospital and town to the lowest point in the frame.
